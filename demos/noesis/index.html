How it works

From paper chaos to clean data in three steps.

1. Connect

Forward emails, connect a shared drive or use our API. Noesis picks up every new document automatically.

2. Understand

Our models read layout, language and context, and check every value against your rules and past documents.

3. Deliver

Verified data flows into your ERP, accounting or spreadsheets. Anything unusual is flagged for a human.

Welcome

Twenty-two rooms, one view, and nowhere you need to be.

Solenne sits halfway between the village and the sky, in a 1920s villa restored stone by stone. Breakfast arrives on your balcony, lemons ripen on the terrace, and the sea changes colour every hour.

— Giulia & Marco, your hosts

Experiences

The coast, at your own pace.

The cliff pool

Sun loungers, lemon granita and the best view in Positano, from 8 am to sunset.

Spa in the rock

Treatments with local citrus and sea salt in a vaulted grotto beneath the villa.

Boat to Capri

A private gozzo with skipper, swimming stops and lunch at a hidden cove.

Breakfast, anywhere

On your balcony, by the pool or on the terrace, served until noon.

La Terrazza

Dinner where the lemon trees end.

A short menu of what the fishermen and farmers bring each morning, cooked simply and served on the terrace as the village lights come on.
